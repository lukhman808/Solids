# Solids - app idea

solids is a fitness or workout tracker where you can note down how many weights you lifted today, how many reps , whats your bodyweight today, how much protien you ate and many things like that. and it shows how you are progressing in that using graphs, it also has a gamification element where you earn "milestone cards" when you complete certain milestones, and it also shows how your calender looks like after you started like a streak calender.
so there are three "tabs"dashboard, sessions, calender, and profile, it will be shown in that order in the bottom navigation bar

## Dashboard
this is where you can see your progress on the milestone cards and also where you can see the plots of your progression
